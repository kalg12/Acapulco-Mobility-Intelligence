# Research Proposal

## Project Title

Acapulco Mobility Intelligence:
A Data-Driven Analysis of Urban Transportation.

## Research Context

Urban mobility is an important component of economic
and social development.

In Acapulco, traditional taxi services and digital
ride-hailing platforms form part of an ongoing
discussion about transportation accessibility,
regulation, service quality, and competition.

This research seeks to contribute empirical evidence
to that discussion.

## Research Problem

There is a need for independently collected,
comparable, and reproducible information about
transportation prices and accessibility in Acapulco.

## Main Research Question

How do transportation costs and service availability
differ between traditional taxi services and digital
ride-hailing platforms in Acapulco?

## General Objective

To analyze transportation prices and accessibility
in Acapulco through statistical methods and
data visualization.

## Specific Objectives

1. Identify relevant public transportation datasets.
2. Define representative transportation routes.
3. Collect comparable transportation fare observations.
4. Analyze differences across routes and time periods.
5. Evaluate transportation accessibility.
6. Publish reproducible findings.

## Initial Hypothesis

Transportation prices and service availability
vary according to transportation modality,
geographic location, and time of day.

## Research Approach

Quantitative, observational, and exploratory.

## Current Status

Research design in progress.

No data has been collected or analyzed yet.
