# Acapulco Mobility Intelligence 🚕

### Urban Mobility Research Through Data Science

An open-source research project focused on analyzing urban mobility,
transportation costs, accessibility, and ride-hailing services
in Acapulco, Guerrero, Mexico.

---

## 📌 About the Project

Acapulco Mobility Intelligence is an independent data science
initiative that aims to understand urban transportation through
reproducible research, statistical analysis, and data visualization.

The project examines traditional taxi services and digital
ride-hailing platforms using publicly available information
and original research data.

The goal is to generate evidence-based insights that contribute
to a better understanding of mobility in Acapulco.

## 🎯 Research Objectives

1. Analyze transportation costs across different areas of Acapulco.
2. Compare traditional taxi services and ride-hailing platforms.
3. Investigate transportation accessibility and availability.
4. Explore mobility patterns using public datasets.
5. Develop interactive dashboards to communicate findings.
6. Publish reproducible analyses and research methodologies.

## ❓ Research Questions

- How do transportation costs vary across Acapulco?
- What differences exist between traditional taxis and digital platforms?
- How does transportation availability vary by location and time?
- What factors influence transportation preferences?
- What can public data reveal about urban mobility in Acapulco?

## 🔬 Research Methodology

The project will follow a reproducible research workflow:

1. Research question definition
2. Data source identification
3. Data collection
4. Data cleaning and validation
5. Exploratory data analysis
6. Statistical analysis
7. Visualization and interpretation
8. Publication of findings

All results will distinguish observed data, estimates,
and interpretations.

## 📊 Data Sources

Potential sources include:

- INEGI
- Government of Guerrero
- Government of Acapulco
- Public transportation statistics
- Transparency requests
- Voluntary surveys
- Independently collected fare observations

Data sources will be documented and verified before use.

## 🔐 Data Ethics

This project is committed to responsible data practices.

- No personal information will be published without authorization.
- Sensitive information will be excluded from public datasets.
- Research limitations will be clearly documented.
- Results will not be presented as causal without appropriate evidence.
- Data collection will respect applicable laws and platform policies.
